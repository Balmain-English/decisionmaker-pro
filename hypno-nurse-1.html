Clinical Communication Simulator

Professional B1 Medical English Training

Welcome to St. Jude’s Medical Centre. You are Nurse Alex, specialized in patient relaxation and pain control. Over three days, you will manage 12 clinical situations, 2 sudden emergencies, and face extra puzzles.

Simulation Rules:
  • Score: Progressive points based on the quality of your communication. Maximum score is hidden.
  • Resources: You must manage Time, Budget, and Patience. If any drop to zero, the game ends immediately.
  • Scoring:
    • 🟩 Excellent (+3 pts) - Ideal clinical English. Boosts resources.
    • 🟦 Good (+2 pts) - Clear and safe. Minor resource cost.
    • 🟧 Average (+1 pt) - Too casual or passive. Moderate resource cost.
    • 🟥 Bad (-1 pt) - Unprofessional. High resource cost. You must try again!
  • Bonus & Puzzles: Solve clinical riddles to earn clues for a final reward. Bonus scenarios may appear if you miss points early on.
