The Hypno-Nurse Chronicles 🌀

A Professional Communication Simulation (B1/B2 English)

Welcome to St. Jude’s Medical Centre! You are Nurse Alex, a highly skilled specialized nurse in Hypno-analgesia & Surgical Hypnosis. Your job is to prepare patients for minor surgeries and painful procedures using hypnotic trance and suggestion instead of (or alongside) heavy chemical anaesthesia.

Over the course of one busy week, you will face 12 routine and delicate professional scenarios, plus 2 unexpected Emergency Situations. Read carefully! Every response seems plausible, but subtle differences will test your communication, empathy, and clinical judgment.

Game Rules & Scoring System:
  • 12 Standard Scenarios: Choose the most effective, professional response.
  • 🟩 Excellent (+3 pts): Ideal combination of empathy, clarity, and patient safety.
  • 🟦 Good (+2 pts): Professional and clear, though slightly rigid or missing therapeutic warmth.
  • 🟧 Average (+1 pt): Technically safe, but passive or mildly dismissive.
  • 🟥 Bad (-1 pt): Counterproductive, clumsy, or unprofessional. Costs 1 point and requires a retry!
  • 🚨 2 Emergency Situations: High-risk clinical events! Excellent (+2 pts), Good (+1 pt), Average (0 pts), or a critical error leading to GAME OVER!
  • Maximum Score: 40 Points (12 × 3 + 2 × 2).
