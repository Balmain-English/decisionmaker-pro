Module 1: Professional IT Context

IT Collaboration in Health Garments

As an IT / CRM Specialist in a medical clothing company, you connect technology with human needs. Your CRM and ERP systems hold essential data for Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance, Logistics, Production, and Clinical Quality Assurance.

Good IT teamwork isn't just about writing code or fixing database bugs—it is about understanding business processes, communicating technical concepts simply, and helping non-technical colleagues serve medical patients effectively.

📊 Core IT Teamworking Competencies in Health Garments

1. Cross-Departmental Communication

Translating complex CRM architectures and data security requirements into clear, simple business terms for Sales and HR.

2. Non-Technical User Empathy

Understanding daily pain points in garment sizing, shipping logs, and billing without dismissing non-technical user frustration.

3. Collaborative Problem-Solving

Finding safe, creative technical compromises that protect system stability while satisfying urgent sales and production goals.

4. Data Integrity & Compliance

Maintaining strict patient data privacy (GDPR) and accurate medical billing codes without slowing down operational workflows.
