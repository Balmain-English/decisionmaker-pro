📊 Key Teamwork Competencies in Health Garments

1. Cross-Disciplinary Communication

Explaining technical fabric or sewing constraints clearly to medical staff and listening to clinical needs without judgment.

2. Empathy for Patient Needs

Prioritizing user comfort, skin sensitivity, and independence when making manufacturing or design adjustments.

3. Early Error Transparency

Reporting pattern errors or stitch defects immediately to protect batch quality and save team time.

4. Constructive Compromise

Finding balanced solutions between strict production deadlines and custom clinical modifications.
