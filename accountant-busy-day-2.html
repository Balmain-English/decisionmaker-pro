A Busy Year in the Life of a Management Accountant

You are Alex Chen, Management Accountant at Enigma Forge Ltd., a fast-growing escape room company. New rooms are being built, a rival is circling and the board wants answers. Every decision you make will affect the company’s financial health — and its survival.

What you will practise

Financial vocabulary in context, reading business scenarios, and choosing — and justifying — the most professional course of action. Three discussion questions appear along the way: pause and talk them through with a partner. (About 20–25 minutes.)

How to play
  1. Read each scenario and the key vocabulary above it.
  2. Choose one of the options. The order changes every time.
  3. Read the feedback to find out why the answer was strong or weak, and compare it with the other options.
How you are scored
Excellent — the best professional response+3
Good — sensible, but not the strongest+2
Weak — flawed reasoning+1
Poor — you lose a point and must choose again−1
Emergencies: when a crisis hits, you get only three options and one of them ends the game. Choose carefully — the best response is worth +2.
